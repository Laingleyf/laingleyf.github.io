Laingley Freemyer

Growing up in Washington, D.C. I have always been a sports fan. I started my love for sports through the Washington Capitals. Following Alex Ovechkin and growing my love for Tj Oshie, I knew I wanted to work in sports. i wasn't positive I wanted to be a broadcaster until I interned for the Washington Capitals. Shadowing their halftime and pre-game and post-game broadcaster, Alexa Landestoy, opened my eyes into how amazing the sports media industry is.

Following my internship I returned back to my high school where I created the "Field Athletic Internship" where I would interview different athletes and eventually took over the offical athletic instagram of my high school. DOing this for two years taught me how to create graphics, interview students and start my content journey.

Other hobbies I enjoy is pottery, watching sports for fun, being outside. I am both a mountains person and a lake person as I have also grown up in Northern Michigan. I hope to one day end up in the midwest either Chicago or Detroit but would also like to spend time in the warmth like Colorado or Florida.
